## Design Context

### Users

Hiring managers, engineering leads, founders, and potential collaborators visiting Tomás Yañez’s portfolio to understand his experience, evaluate his range, see selected work, and contact him. The experience should make a strong creative impression without obscuring the substance of his engineering career.

### Brand Personality

Creative, elegant, alive. A technically rigorous engineer with a curious, expressive point of view. The portfolio may be more colorful and experimental than Fabersoft’s corporate site while retaining a serious undertone.

### Aesthetic Direction

Cinematic editorial composition with solid Fabersoft-derived cyan, orange, violet, emerald, sky, and amber surfaces; warm tinted neutrals; oversized expressive typography; soft organic corners; asymmetry; and purposeful scroll-driven morphing. Use the supplied portraits and project imagery. Avoid a generic white SaaS landing page, excessive glassmorphism, repetitive card grids, neon-on-dark clichés, and copying Fabersoft’s prominent components.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with one memorable cinematic moment: the hero portrait morphs from a framed image into a full-screen scene as the visitor scrolls.
2. Use solid color as a structural element, not decoration; every chapter has a clear palette and emotional register.
3. Pair expressive motion with readable, useful content and fast, direct contact paths.
4. Build original compositions around soft geometry and editorial typography rather than assembling a library of prefabricated components.
5. Treat responsiveness, reduced motion, keyboard access, and graceful fallbacks as part of the visual design.
